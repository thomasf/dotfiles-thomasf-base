IBM Carbon Color Palette Specification
Creator: IBM (https://github.com/carbon-design-system/carbon)
Source: @carbon/themes (g100, white) and @carbon/colors
License: Apache-2.0

Each color family has grades 10 (lightest) to 100 (darkest); a grade has the
same contrast in every family.

  support-warning #f1c21b   yellow 30
